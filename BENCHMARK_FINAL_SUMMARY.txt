CRYSTALLINE TIER 1 — HISTORICAL BENCHMARK SUMMARY

Status: HISTORICAL / QUARANTINED
Original artifact date: 2026-03-24

The original benchmark suite measured spectral analysis, filtering, matrix multiplication, linear solves, and convolution against selected numerical libraries. Those results remain in the repository for provenance.

Founder-audit disposition:

- The benchmark artifacts mix generations and environments.
- Some reported comparisons were not semantically or methodologically sufficient for broad conclusions.
- Historical timing tables do not establish a universal speed-up factor.
- Historical timing tables do not establish production readiness.
- Tier 1 is CPU-only; the historical reports provide no evidence of GPU execution.
- Future GPU speed-up estimates are not evidence and are intentionally removed from the public position.

Replacement gate:

1. Independently validate numerical correctness.
2. Pin the exact commit and dependency versions.
3. Record hardware, OS, BLAS/LAPACK, and thread configuration.
4. Use identical mathematical semantics across implementations.
5. Record warm-up policy, repetitions, raw observations, and uncertainty.
6. Separate cold-start from steady-state results.

Until that gate is completed, performance statements remain operation- and environment-specific.

See benchmarks/BENCHMARK_REPORT.md for the retained historical record.
