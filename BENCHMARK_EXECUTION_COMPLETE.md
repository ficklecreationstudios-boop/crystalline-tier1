## Audit disposition

The original execution report mixed benchmark generations and environments and used conclusions that were broader than the evidence supported. Therefore its timing tables and qualitative rankings must not be treated as current capability claims.

In particular, this artifact does **not** establish:

- universal CPU acceleration;
- a fixed competitiveness percentage against NumPy, SciPy, PyTorch, or other libraries;
- production readiness;
- GPU execution in Tier 1; or
- future GPU speed-up forecasts.

The authoritative historical interpretation is `benchmarks/BENCHMARK_REPORT.md`. A replacement benchmark is gated on independent numerical correctness and reproducible methodology.
